How to play

Guide your snake to food, grow longer, and avoid collisions. Opening this guide pauses play or the countdown. Close it with × or Escape, then choose Resume to continue.

1. Start and steer

  • On Home, enter your name, then choose Map and Play just above Let’s play. Choose Google, Facebook, or Play without login; only guests are asked for a player name. Two players also choose a team name. Every new round and rematch begins with 1 → 2 → 3 → GO!
  • Solo / AI Arena: use WASD or arrow keys.
  • Two players: Player 1 uses WASD; Player 2 uses arrow keys. On touchscreens, use the labeled direction pads. Swiping the board steers Player 1.
  • You cannot reverse directly into yourself. Two legal turns can be queued; you can choose your first turn during the countdown.
  • Space: start, pause, or resume. Escape: pause or close an open panel. R: restart. Home: leave the round and return to setup. In Settings → More options, enable break auto-pause and choose 1–60 minutes (default 2). Pauses, Home, countdown, and new rounds reset this uninterrupted-play timer; Resume starts it again. The red-on-black digital clock shows play time and the next break; the final ten seconds are spoken when sound and browser speech are available.
  • Maps, player count, and food density lock during play, countdown, and pause. Return Home to change them. Skins and backdrops can be changed without resetting a round.

2. Maps and winning

  • Classic: edges wrap to the opposite side; there are no fixed obstacles. Mushrooms and healing herbs appear only here.
  • Obstacles: solid outer walls, striped barriers, and square stone blocks marked ×. These are hazards, not food.
  • Maze: solid outer walls and winding passages. No mushrooms or herbs.
  • A wall, hazard, your own body, or another snake’s body ends an unshielded run. A non-growing tail vacates its cell; growing or shield-stopped tails stay solid.
  • Solo: fill every open cell to win. Two-player team: choose a team name and work together. Both players’ points add to the team score. Any unshielded crash, including a teammate collision, ends the team run; fill the board together to win.
  • AI Arena: outlast all bots to win. Your own crash ends the round; crashed bots disappear. Hungry pursues food, Cautious favors escape space, and Collector favors power-ups.
  • In AI Arena, a full board compares surviving scores; ties draw. In team play, filling the board is a shared win. Moves resolve together, without a first-player advantage.

3. Food and special items

🐀 Rat · 🐇 Rabbit · 🐸 Frog
Each catch gives +1 point and one body segment. Rats scurry, rabbits hop, and frogs jump to clear locations. Nearby animals stay put so you can finish your catch.
★ Golden food
+5 points and one segment. Appears after 25–55 successful moves and disappears after 50 moves if untouched. Its banner shows the remaining time. It does not advance animal-based levels.
🛡 Shield
Protects against one collision for up to 8 active seconds. A dangerous move stops in place and consumes the shield. Turn to safety before the next tick! It does not let you pass through walls or snakes. No points or growth.
⏱ Slow motion
For 6 active seconds, all snakes move at two-thirds of their current speed: time per cell becomes 1.5× longer. Your level does not change. No points or growth.
🍄 Mushroom — Classic only
+50 points and one segment, but directional input reverses: left ↔ right and up ↔ down, including keyboard, touch pads, and swipes. Warnings and reversed pad labels identify the effect. It lasts until you collect a healing herb; it does not wear off by itself.
More mushrooms give another +50 but do not toggle controls back or postpone the herb.
🌿 Healing herb — Classic only
Appears after 5 active seconds following mushroom ingestion, when a clear cell is available. Collect it to restore normal controls and clear queued turns. No points or growth.

Shield and clock pickups appear automatically, one at a time, starting after seven active seconds. Untouched power-ups disappear after ten seconds. Pause and countdown freeze gameplay timers.

4. Levels and speed

Level 1 requires 10 rats, 10 rabbits, and 10 frogs caught by humans. Each later level needs 5 more of each animal: 15 each in Level 2, 20 each in Level 3, and so on. These are fresh targets for each level, not cumulative round totals.

In Solo and AI Arena, only your catches count. In local two-player games, both humans contribute together. AI catches, special items, and score bonuses never increase the level. Level progress resets each summer; round totals and your score remain until the round ends.

When the target is reached, “Level completed — Winter is coming” appears. Choose Find shelter and steer into the labeled hollow TREE DEN at the edge. Gentle snow falls and up to three spaced-out frozen × blocks appear; avoid them like other walls. The direct route and space around your head stay clear. Both teammates must enter in co-op; AI snakes rest automatically. Watch your snake slowly slide into the tree, tail last. Reduced-motion mode skips snowfall and shortens the animation. Summer then starts the next level with a three-segment snake, preserved score, and higher speed.

5. Chains, goals, and records

  • Catch another animal within 12 active seconds to keep your chain. Every third animal catch earns +1 bonus point, without extra growth. Each human has an independent chain; special items do not add to or refresh it.
  • Optional goals rotate between catching one of each animal, surviving 60 active seconds, and building a three-animal chain. Goals add no points and are never required to win.
  • Round medals celebrate Balanced diet, Survivor, Shield save, Catch rhythm, and a new category record. Results show each human’s score, survival time, animals, chain, and medals.
  • Best scores and champion names are saved separately for each map + play type—for example, Classic · Solo and Maze · Solo. Bots never replace human records; ties keep the first champion.
  • Choose Rematch to keep your setup and begin a fresh countdown, or Home to change names, map, or play type.
